# Design Spec: PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 & Legal Protection

**Goal:** Formally update the application license from permissive MIT to PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 to legally prohibit third-party monetization while ensuring individual pharmacies can freely use the app for their operations.

---

## 1. Background & Rationale
- The application was previously under MIT, which permitted third parties to resell or commercialize the app.
- The platform is designed as an offline-first inventory & POS solution for individual pharmacies.
- The creator retains exclusive rights to monetize, commercialize, or issue commercial licenses.

---

## 2. Key Terms & Enforcement

### Permitted Uses
- Individual pharmacy operators may download, install, run, and use the platform free of charge for their own daily business operations (POS sales, stock management, drug inventory tracking).

### Prohibited Uses
- Third parties MAY NOT sell, resell, charge subscription/SaaS fees, bundle into paid software packages, or monetize the code/app in any manner.

### Creator Rights
- The original authors/copyright holders retain 100% exclusive rights to monetize, host paid cloud services, or issue commercial enterprise licenses.
