# Privacy transparency

## Problem

The application has no in-app explanation of local data, optional Google Drive
backups, retention, or safe device erasure. Its help dialog also bypasses ARB
localization.

## Acceptance criteria

- Help is localized in Indonesian and English.
- Users can read what stays on-device, what can be sent to Drive, and how to
  remove local data safely.
- Copy does not claim legal retention policy or imply Drive is configured.
