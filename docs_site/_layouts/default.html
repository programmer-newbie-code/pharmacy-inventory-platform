Key Capabilities

Engineered for accuracy, BPOM compliance, and lightning-fast POS checkout.

🛒

FEFO Stock Allocation

Automatic First-Expired, First-Out (FEFO) stock deduction during POS checkout ensures earlier expiring batches are sold first.

💊

Controlled Drug Verification

Enforces mandatory doctor name, patient name, and prescription checks before checking out controlled medicines.

⚠️

Expiry & Low Stock Alerts

Color-coded watchlists for batches expiring in 30, 60, 90, or 180 days, plus automatic reorder threshold notifications.

👥

Role-Based Access

Granular employee permission roles (`Admin`, `Pharmacist`, `Cashier`, `Auditor`) with SHA-256 password hashing.

💾

Atomic Backup & Restore

Full JSON database export and single-transaction atomic restoration with comprehensive audit logging.

📄

BPOM Regulatory CSV Export

Instant structured CSV exports of prescription and sales transactions for BPOM and ministry compliance reporting.
